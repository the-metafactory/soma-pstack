# Pack policy bindings

Unattended runs keep a decision trail. Use [show-me-your-work](skills/show-me-your-work/SKILL.md) for the canonical log, end-of-run transcript audit, and cross-model review ending in Attention.

This binds the pack's skill to Soma's decision-trail policy; it does not replace the principal's policy. Decision source: [soma#1084](https://github.com/the-metafactory/soma/issues/1084), moved to pack node #5.
